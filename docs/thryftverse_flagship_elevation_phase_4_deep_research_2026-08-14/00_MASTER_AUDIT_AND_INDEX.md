# Thryftverse Phase 4 — Deep Research Flagship Elevation

**Audit date:** 2026-08-14  
**Target branch:** `feat/product-detail-contract-media-device-closure`

This is a full next-elevation package, not a short backlog.

## Central conclusion

Thryftverse now has much of the necessary functionality. Its remaining distance from Pinterest/Instagram/Depop/eBay-level *perceived* quality is increasingly caused by:

- feature visibility;
- composition;
- control density;
- media hierarchy;
- department identity;
- progressive disclosure;
- copy economy;
- platform material/navigation semantics;
- lack of native screenshot closure.
