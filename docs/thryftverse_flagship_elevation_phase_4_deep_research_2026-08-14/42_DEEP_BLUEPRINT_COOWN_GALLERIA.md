# Deep blueprint — Co-Own, Galleria and Due Diligence

Co-Own is a special product family. It cannot look like:
- a normal marketplace listing;
- a stock trading app;
- a settings page.

It needs the emotional authority of a catalogue and the factual depth of an investment/evidence product.

---

# 1. Product split

## Galleria
Purpose:
**desire, context, culture, collection.**

## Co-Own Hub
Purpose:
**availability, positions, market access.**

## Asset Detail
Purpose:
**object + ownership proposition.**

## Due Diligence
Purpose:
**evidence and legal/financial confidence.**

## Portfolio
Purpose:
**my holdings.**

A user should never wonder why these are separate.

---

# 2. Galleria visual architecture

Current:
- hero editorial;
- collection rail;
- featured masonry;
- editorial list.

This is directionally right.

### Problem
Fixed reusable card proportions can make “editorial” feel like a set of components.

### Target
More magazine logic:
- hero;
- negative space;
- variable collection scale;
- large text interruption;
- image pairs;
- featured object close-ups.

### Hero
One only.

Do not put:
- 3 badges;
- valuation;
- CTA button;
inside hero.

Overlay:
eyebrow, title, author/read time.

### Collections
Instead of all 200pt cards:
allow:
- one wide featured collection;
- smaller following covers;
- collage cover.

Curator line below.

### Featured objects
Masonry is good.
Metadata:
- collection;
- title;
- valuation/reference.

Avoid market allocation badges here.

# 3. Co-Own Hub

Current rows:
- highlights;
- tabs;
- positions;
- instruments header;
- grid rows.

### New order if holder
```
Header
Your positions
Active/New issues/Watchlist
Market grid
```

### New order if non-holder
```
Header
market highlight / education only if first time
Active/New issues/Watchlist
Market grid
```

Do not always put generic highlights before existing holdings.

### Education
One-time or progressive.
Never permanent “what is Co-Own” card for experienced user.

---

# 4. Instrument card

Must answer:
- what;
- per-unit price;
- available;
- state.

Example:
```
[image]
Rolex GMT-Master II
1ZE 1.24 / unit
220 of 1,000 available
```

Allocation visualization:
small line/bar only if useful.

Do not display:
- holders;
- jurisdiction;
- settlement mode;
- multiple badges
all at once.

---

# 5. Asset Detail

Preserve current Phase 3 hierarchy.

### Media
Treat as museum object.
Use:
- full bleed / generous stage;
- additional provenance photos;
- detail shots.

### Identity
- title;
- legal vehicle as secondary;
- issuer;
- per-unit price;
- availability;
- market open.

### “About”
Short story/provenance.

### Market & my position
Non-holder:
- reference;
- bid/ask;
- market depth disclosure.

Holder:
- owned units;
- position value;
- P&L if data authoritative;
- Buy/Sell.

### Due diligence entry
One large but flat row:
`Due diligence`
`Authentication, custody, valuation, rights & risk`

No attempt to summarize all evidence on Asset Detail.

---

# 6. Due Diligence — convert from settings rows to dossier

## Section 1: Provenance
Timeline:
```
2019  Acquired by ...
2023  Appraised ...
2026  Entered custody ...
```

Evidence document thumbnails inline.

## 2: Authentication
Verifier identity.
Date.
Result.
Evidence images/serials.

## 3: Condition
Grade.
Inspector.
Inspection date.
Photo evidence.

## 4: Custody & insurance
Custodian.
Location level allowed by privacy/security.
Policy coverage summary.
Evidence/terms.

## 5: Valuation
Large:
`£124,000`
`Appraised 12 Jul 2026 · Sotheby's...`

Below:
methodology/comparables.

## 6: Ownership structure
Diagram:
`Asset -> SPV/vehicle -> issued units -> holder rights`

This is much more comprehensible than 8 text rows.

## 7: Rights
Compact table:
- distributions;
- vote;
- exit;
- fees.

TBC rows:
gray, reason/ETA.

## 8: Risks
Plain language.
No red panic card unless severe current event.

## 9: Audit history
Timeline of changes.

---

# 7. Advanced market surface

Order book and candle chart:
hidden under:
`Market details`

This creates progressive expertise.

When expanded:
- bid/ask;
- spread;
- order book;
- volume/chart.

Do not make novice user see this before the object's story.

---

# 8. Co-Own psychology

### Avoid crypto cues
- neon green/red;
- constant price movement;
- micro-tickers;
- “moon” urgency;
- pseudo-performance.

### Prefer
- catalogue typography;
- neutral finance;
- clear numbers;
- evidence;
- stable layouts.

---

# 9. Galleria vs Pinterest reference

Use Pinterest for:
- media density;
- collections;
- inspiration.

Do **not** turn Galleria into Pinterest clone.
Galleria needs more breathing room and curatorial hierarchy because the inventory is rarer/higher-consideration.

# 11. Fail conditions

- allocation bar on every Galleria image;
- Due Diligence still looks like Settings;
- 4+ rounded cards in a row;
- object image smaller than financial dashboard above fold;
- market movement fabricated for visual liveliness;
- “To be confirmed” hidden or made to look verified.
