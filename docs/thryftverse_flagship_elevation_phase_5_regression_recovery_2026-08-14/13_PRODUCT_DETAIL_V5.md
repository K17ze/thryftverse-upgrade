## Preserve Phase 3/4 truth

Do not restore card soup.

## Problem to solve

The danger after Phase 4 is over-minimalism:
trust and commerce context can become too quiet.

## First decision sequence

1. Desire — media.
2. Identity — brand/title/size/condition/price.
3. Confidence — seller + 2–3 trust facts.
4. Action — Buy / Offer.
5. Detail.
6. Logistics/protection.
7. Social evidence.
8. Continuation.

## Richness comes from media and typography, not boxes

Use:
- high-quality full media;
- structured item facts;
- seller identity;
- contextual trust.

Avoid:
- multiple bland rows with no visual anchor;
- generic icons replacing real evidence.

## Luxury/authenticated item

Add richness through:
- authenticity media;
- serial/details;
- provenance;
- verifier/source;
- condition close-ups.

Not through:
- gold borders;
- extra “Premium” badge.

## Multi-media

eBay currently places listing video within the image gallery. Thryftverse should keep image/video as one coherent media stage.

## Recommendations

Home-like cards should use commerce identity, not the bland Phase 4 image-only treatment.

## Buying details

Shipping/returns/protection can be flat rows, but use actual:
- delivery estimate;
- cost;
- eligibility;
not generic helper copy.

## Acceptance

Minimal UI must not mean the buyer has less confidence than before.
