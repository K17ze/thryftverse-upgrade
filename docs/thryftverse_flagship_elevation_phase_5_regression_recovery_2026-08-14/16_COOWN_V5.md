Keep that as a regression test.

## Roles

Galleria = editorial.
Hub instrument = live market / commerce.
Position = personal financial collection.
Due Diligence = evidence.

Each uses different composition.

## Positions

Horizontal visual rail:
- recognizable object image;
- title;
- owned units / value;
- state.

Do not turn into plain settings rows.

## Hub

Market clarity:
- positions first when holder;
- active/new issues;
- instruments.

## Galleria

Rich media/editorial typography.
No allocation bars on every editorial image.

## Asset Detail

Keep collectible-first.

## Due Diligence

Evidence must look like evidence:
- timeline;
- source;
- verifier;
- documents;
- images;
- dates.

Not repeated metric rows.

## Backend

Strictly preserve:
- unit price truth;
- settlement;
- holdings;
- rights;
- TBC states;
- reconciliation.

## Acceptance

Visual richness may increase, but no synthetic finance metric is added just to make the screen interesting.
