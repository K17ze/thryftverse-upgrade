## Apple current HIG

Search guidance updated June 8, 2026:
- one clearly identifiable search location;
- search can be a dedicated tab/area when browsing and discovery belong together;
- scopes/tokens refine results.

Tab Bar guidance:
- tab bars are for navigation, not actions.

Materials:
- Liquid Glass is a navigation/control layer;
- Apple explicitly advises against using it as content-layer decoration.

Notifications:
- timely, high-value information understood at a glance;
- communication notifications can use people/group identity.

## Android current

June 2026 layout/navigation guidance:
- adapt navigation to window size;
- use rail on larger windows rather than stretching bottom navigation;
- additional low-frequency actions belong in overflow.

## Pinterest 2026

Pinterest continues investing in visual-search discovery infrastructure.
The lesson for Thryftverse:
- image as query;
- visual shopping;
- content-led discovery.

Do not interpret this as “remove all commerce text.”

## Instagram Instants — May 2026

Instants:
- direct camera;
- real-time capture;
- no gallery upload in that mode;
- no pre-share editing beyond caption.

Lesson:
a constrained fast path can coexist with a richer creator.

## Vinted

Current recommendation/search explanations include:
- query;
- expressed filters;
- description;
- condition;
- price;
- image resemblance;
- interaction/personalization.

Lesson:
structured backend item data is a visual/search quality dependency.

## Depop

Current search-rank guidance:
- relevance has the biggest impact;
- brand/category/color/description matter;
- clear, accurate descriptions and multiple photos help;
- suggested price uses comparable sold items.

## eBay

Current listing tools:
- first photo is main search photo;
- up to 24 photos;
- video appears in listing media gallery;
- clean/simplified listing tools.

## Snapchat Groups

Current creation starts from Chat and choosing people.
Member addition is also a people-selection action.

Lesson:
Create Group should be people-first, not username-form-first.

## Reference rule

Borrow cognition and information hierarchy.
Do not clone trademarked styling or exact layouts.
