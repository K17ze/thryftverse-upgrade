# Flagship Release Scorecard

Score 0–10.

## 1. Focal hierarchy
10 = one obvious primary object/action; secondary elements recede; no competing equal-weight cards.

## 2. Surface restraint
10 = borders have purpose; grouping mostly uses type/spacing/alignment; no rectangle soup.

## 3. Information architecture
10 = needed information first; expert data progressive; no duplication.

## 4. Functional truth
10 = every visible control acts on real domain state and survives reload.

## 5. Commerce clarity
10 = costs/outcomes/shipping/fees/protection are understandable and truthful.

## 6. Native interaction
10 = gesture/haptic/motion/keyboard/navigation support the task.

## 7. Media fidelity
10 = correct derivative/crop/focal point; no blur/layout jump; video poster/playback correct.

## 8. State completeness
10 = loading, empty, error, offline, retry, partial and unavailable states preserve context.

## 9. Accessibility
10 = text scaling, contrast, reduce motion, screen reader, focus, target size, non-color state.

## 10. Product signature
10 = recognizably ThryftVerse; premium restraint; social/commerce/ownership coherent; not generic template styling.

# Thresholds

Core:
- mean ≥9.0;
- no dimension <8.0;
- Functional Truth ≥9.5;
- State Completeness ≥9;
- native proof required.

Secondary:
- mean ≥8.5;
- no dimension <7.5.
