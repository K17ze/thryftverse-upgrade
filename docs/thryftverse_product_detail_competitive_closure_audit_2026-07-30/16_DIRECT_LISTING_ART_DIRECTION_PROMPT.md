# Direct Listing Art-Direction Prompt

## Goal

Make Direct feel like desire-first editorial resale with fast, trustworthy purchase decisions.

## Preconditions

P0 Direct contract and canonical media work must be complete.

## Composition

1. Object-safe gallery.
2. Independent title/brand/price block.
3. Condition, size and one essential product-fact line.
4. Evidence-backed seller strip.
5. Buy/offer instrument.
6. One delivery/protection disclosure.
7. Description and facts.
8. Q&A row.
9. Bundle, Seen in Looks, then decision-complete recommendations.
10. State-aware dock.

Avoid placing critical text over arbitrary seller photos. Reduce rounded containers and use typographic/spacing hierarchy.

## Interaction

- Sticky action reflects server capability and reason.
- Offer/checkout unknown outcomes recover idempotently.
- Reserved/unavailable changes arrive while viewing.
- Discovery cards include enough identity to support a decision.
- Sheets return focus and fit large text/keyboard.

## States

Implement live, reserved, offer states, checkout/payment pending, sold, paused, removed, private owner preview, shipping restricted, policy unavailable, partial secondary failures and offline recovery.

## Visual proof

At minimum capture buyer live, seller live, reserved, sold, offer accepted, unavailable and mixed-media states at 320/390/430, light/dark, normal/large text.

## Acceptance

- A missing fact is omitted/explained, never invented.
- The product silhouette is preserved.
- The first viewport establishes object, price, seller confidence and action.
- Direct is compositionally distinct from Auction and Co-Own.
- Full frontend suite and Direct runtime/native tests pass.
