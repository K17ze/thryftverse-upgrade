## Do not lead with all capabilities

First-time buyer:
- interests;
- discover;
- save/follow.

First-time casual seller:
- list first item.

Creator:
- capture first Poster/Look.

Professional seller:
- activate Storefront.

High-value specialist:
- category verification/eligibility progressively.

## Cultural onboarding

Do not use stock photos of generic “luxury lifestyle”.

Use actual platform content with permission and truthful provenance.

## Store migration

After seller verifies:
offer:
- Import inventory;
- Create first collection;
- Share store.

## Success

Activation metric should differ:
- buyer first save/detail;
- seller first published listing;
- creator first published content;
- professional store first live collection.
