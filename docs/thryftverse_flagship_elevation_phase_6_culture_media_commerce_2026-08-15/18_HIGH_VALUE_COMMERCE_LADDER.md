## Why one checkout is not enough

A £40 garment, £12k Birkin, £70k watch, £250k car and £4m yacht have different:
- fraud risk;
- inspection needs;
- logistics;
- taxes;
- payment rails;
- legal ownership;
- insurance;
- negotiation.

## Tier 0 — social / non-commerce
Poster, Look, collection.

## Tier 1 — standard resale
Fashion/accessories.
- normal seller trust;
- payment;
- shipping;
- returns/protection.

## Tier 2 — authenticated luxury
Bags, watches, jewellery.
- seller identity;
- category evidence;
- authenticity route;
- insured shipping;
- higher-value payout controls.

## Tier 3 — specialist high-value
Rare watches, art/collectibles.
- expert review;
- provenance;
- inspection;
- escrow-like flow where legally/operationally supported;
- concierge.

## Tier 4 — brokered assets
Cars/yachts and similar assets.
- enquiry;
- qualified buyer/seller;
- documentation;
- viewing/inspection;
- survey/sea trial for yachts;
- negotiated offer;
- deposit/escrow/closing partners.

Do not show `Buy now £4,000,000` as if it were sneakers.

## UI principle

Discovery remains simple.
Trust complexity unfolds as:
- category;
- price;
- action;
require it.
