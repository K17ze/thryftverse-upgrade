## Listing media recipe

Seller prompts should request:
- full front;
- back;
- sides/base;
- handles/strap;
- interior;
- corners;
- hardware;
- brand stamp;
- serial/date-code evidence where category/model uses it;
- included packaging/accessories;
- every flaw.

## Product Detail

First:
- beautiful media;
- model;
- size;
- colour;
- material;
- price;
- seller.

Then:
`Authenticity & condition`

Evidence:
- verifier/authentication route;
- seller-provided evidence;
- inspection result;
- included accessories;
- condition map.

## Authentication status

Do not use a vague gold “verified” badge.

Use explicit:
- Eligible for authentication;
- Authentication in progress;
- Authenticated;
- Could not authenticate / discrepancy.

## Buyer flow

For high-value eligible:
seller → authenticator/inspection → buyer
or another operational model selected by business/legal review.

## Reference lesson

eBay’s current Authenticity Guarantee makes eligibility/status visible and physically inspects eligible high-value goods. Vestiaire’s quality control compares the actual item against listing details.

Thryftverse should make **evidence itself visible**, not only a badge.
