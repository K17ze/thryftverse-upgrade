## Why yachts need their own architecture

A yacht listing is:
- a complex asset;
- documentation-heavy;
- often broker-mediated;
- inspection/survey dependent;
- location/logistics dependent.

## Discovery

Editorial:
- large photographs;
- video walkthrough;
- location;
- model/year/length;
- asking price.

## Detail

- builder/model;
- year/refit;
- length/beam/draft;
- engines/hours;
- cabins/berths;
- cruising/max speed;
- flag/registration where appropriate;
- location;
- equipment;
- maintenance;
- survey availability;
- broker/seller;
- asking terms.

## Primary CTA

`Enquire`
or
`Request viewing`

not standard Buy Now.

## Qualified workflow

- buyer enquiry;
- broker response;
- viewing;
- offer;
- documentation;
- survey/sea trial;
- negotiation;
- deposit/escrow;
- closing.

## Seller mode

Private seller:
guided listing + option to connect broker.

Broker:
professional account + multiple listings + lead pipeline.

## Reference lesson

Yacht marketplaces currently position brokers as handling valuation, marketing, negotiation, paperwork, survey/sea-trial and closing. Thryftverse should treat this as a **service workflow**, not just an expensive Product Detail page.
