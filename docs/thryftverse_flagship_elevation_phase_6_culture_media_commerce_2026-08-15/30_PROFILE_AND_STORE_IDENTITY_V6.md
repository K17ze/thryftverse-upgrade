## Personal profile

Identity:
- cover;
- avatar;
- name/handle;
- bio;
- relationship;
- Shop;
- Looks;
- Posters/Media;
- About.

Owner utilities recede.

## Seller store mode

Storefront is a mode/role, not a second duplicated profile.

Business/pro seller gains:
- Store tab;
- Collections;
- Drops;
- policies;
- location;
- verification.

## Grid

The supplied references demonstrate the strength of a nearly edge-to-edge 3-column media grid.

For social Poster/Look/profile content:
- tiny gutters;
- tiny media-type marker;
- no caption overlay in grid;
- high-resolution physical-pixel thumbnails.

For Shop:
- 2/3-column commerce grid with identity/price depending context.

## Pinned content

Allow a small number of pinned:
- collection;
- Poster;
- product/drop.

Do not turn profile top into widgets.

## Trust

Public trust becomes:
- verification;
- transaction history/rating;
- category credential
where relevant.

Do not expose private wealth/status scoring.
