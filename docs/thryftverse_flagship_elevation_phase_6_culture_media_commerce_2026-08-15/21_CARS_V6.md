## Discovery

Cars can appear as cultural content in Poster/Look/editorial, but a sellable vehicle opens a specialist detail.

## Listing facts

- make/model/variant;
- year;
- mileage;
- powertrain;
- transmission;
- exterior/interior;
- service;
- ownership;
- accident/repair disclosure;
- location;
- registration/VIN privacy-safe proof;
- finance/tax fields by country.

## Media recipe

- 4 corners;
- sides;
- front/rear;
- wheels/tyres;
- interior front/rear;
- dashboard;
- odometer;
- engine bay;
- trunk;
- defects;
- cold start/video;
- walkaround.

## Transaction

High value:
- enquiry;
- offer;
- viewing;
- inspection;
- deposit;
- closing/payment partner;
rather than instant standard checkout unless category/value/legal design supports it.

## Storefront

Dealer/specialist seller can have:
- inventory;
- sold archive;
- collection;
- services;
- location;
- trust/licensing.

## International

Do not pretend one tax/registration flow covers every market.
Build country capability flags.
