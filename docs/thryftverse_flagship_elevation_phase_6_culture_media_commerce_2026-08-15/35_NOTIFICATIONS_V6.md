## Phase 6 art direction

Notifications are operational, not editorial.

Use:
- Needs attention;
- chronological;
- one filter.

## High-value

High-priority examples:
- authentication discrepancy;
- inspection scheduled;
- offer deadline;
- payment/escrow action;
- outbid.

These should be visible, concise and precise.

## Social

Likes/follows/reactions aggregate quietly.

## Store

Seller action:
- order;
- enquiry;
- evidence requested;
- stock issue.

## Anti-slop

No six tabs, no badge on every row, no animation for ordinary notifications.
