## Worldwide product means more than currency conversion

Support capability by:
- market;
- category;
- shipping;
- payment;
- tax;
- verification;
- prohibited/restricted item policy.

## Currency

Local display + canonical transaction currency.

## Units

Metric/imperial by locale/category.

## Translation

Listings:
seller text may translate, but original accessible.

Creator artwork:
do not automatically translate artwork.

## Dates/time

Auction/drop/viewing local timezone explicit.

## High-value

Cars/yachts/art can have country-specific ownership/export/import requirements.

Capability flags decide available workflows.

## Storefront

Seller can set:
- shipping destinations;
- languages;
- base currency;
- returns/policies.
