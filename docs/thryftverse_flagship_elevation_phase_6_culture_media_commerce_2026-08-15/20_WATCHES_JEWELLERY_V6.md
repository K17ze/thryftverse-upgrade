## Watch listing facts

- brand;
- model;
- reference;
- year if known;
- case size/material;
- movement;
- dial;
- bracelet/strap;
- box;
- papers;
- service history;
- condition;
- seller ownership evidence.

## Media

- straight dial;
- side/crown;
- caseback;
- bracelet/clasp;
- reference/serial evidence with privacy policy;
- wrist/scale;
- accessories;
- defects;
- time-setting/running proof if useful.

## High-value trust

At thresholds:
- authentication/Certified eligibility;
- insured shipping;
- signature;
- payout hold;
- expert review.

## Product Detail

Use horological hierarchy:
- object/media;
- model/ref;
- price;
- condition;
- included set;
- authenticity;
- service/provenance;
- seller;
- transaction.

Do not make it look like crypto trading.

## Jewellery

Add:
- metal;
- stone;
- carat/weight;
- dimensions;
- hallmarks;
- certificates;
- condition;
- resizing/history.

## Reference lesson

Chrono24's current Buyer Protection combines escrow-like fund handling, ownership checks and transaction safeguards. eBay Authenticity Guarantee applies category/value-specific watch and jewellery authentication.

The exact Thryftverse threshold/business process must be designed with legal/payment/insurance partners rather than copied.
