## Objective

A seller currently using social media as their “store” needs:
- cultural identity;
- audience;
- content;
- DMs;
but also operational commerce.

Thryftverse should make a seller profile become a real **Storefront**.

## Storefront first screen

- cover/campaign media;
- avatar/store identity;
- concise location/category;
- Follow;
- Message;
- trust level;
- Shop;
- Looks;
- Drops/Collections;
- About.

Do not begin with seller metrics.

## Shop

Filters:
- New
- Available
- Sold/archive if seller chooses.

Grid can be visually high density.

## Collections

Seller-authored:
- Summer in Capri
- Watches
- Objects
- Archive
- Evening
- Yacht accessories

Collections are commerce/editorial hybrids.

## Drops

Scheduled/new release:
- campaign media;
- items;
- countdown only when real;
- follow/notify.

## Lookbook

Seller's Poster/Look content can tag current inventory.

## About

- seller story;
- location;
- shipping;
- returns;
- authenticity;
- business seller information where legally required.

## Store identity

Allow:
- cover;
- avatar;
- short description;
- store name;
- colour accent with strict restraint;
- typography only for campaign/editorial modules, not transaction UI.

Do not allow arbitrary CSS/theme that destroys marketplace trust.

## URL/share

Stable public store link.
Deep links into collection/item.

## Operations

Storefront status comes from Seller Hub:
- stock;
- orders;
- messages;
- payouts;
- authentication;
- scheduled drops.

## Success metric

A luxury reseller should prefer sharing their Thryftverse storefront link rather than telling buyers to DM for price/availability.
