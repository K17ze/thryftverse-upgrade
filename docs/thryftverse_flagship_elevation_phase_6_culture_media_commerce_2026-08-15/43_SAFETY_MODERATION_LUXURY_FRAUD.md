## High-value attraction increases adversarial pressure

Threats:
- counterfeit;
- stolen goods;
- fake provenance;
- chargeback;
- account takeover;
- off-platform payment;
- social engineering;
- synthetic/fake media;
- serial-document theft.

## Media lifecycle

Preserve current scan/moderation infrastructure.

## Evidence

Sensitive serials/documents:
- private evidence storage;
- redacted public display;
- role-based specialist access;
- audit log.

## Seller risk

Risk systems must not use protected characteristics or lifestyle imagery as a proxy for trust.

Use:
- account security;
- transaction history;
- evidence;
- payment/identity;
- operational signals.

## Messaging

Warn on:
- requests for off-platform payment;
- credential sharing;
- suspicious links
without making every conversation alarming.

## Authenticity

Do not allow seller self-selection of `Authenticated`.

Only platform/system-approved process can set.
