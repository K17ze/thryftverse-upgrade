## Target Look default

Canvas, close, undo/redo, Next, one contextual bottom rail. No extra layout rails.

## Target Poster default

Full canvas, close, undo/redo, Next, compact creation rail. Timeline only when video/multiple clips or explicitly invoked.

## Rule

Permanent visibility requires at least one of: high-frequency use, essential state communication, necessary task completion, or high cost of later discovery.
