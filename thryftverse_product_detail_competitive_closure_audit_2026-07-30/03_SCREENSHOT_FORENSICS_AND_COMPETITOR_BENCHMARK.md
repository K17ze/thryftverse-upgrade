# Screenshot Forensics and Competitor Benchmark

## What the supplied references demonstrate

### Pinterest: immersive intent

The Pinterest reference gives the image the overwhelming majority of the first viewport. Social actions are peripheral, and related discovery continues in a dense masonry field. The lesson is not to copy Pinterest’s layout; it is that an image-led product can establish desire before explaining the transaction.

### eBay: decision completeness

The eBay references use a large image, visible thumbnail access, seller context, explicit price/finance/delivery, vertically legible actions, urgency, facts, description and related merchandise. It is not visually luxurious, but a buyer can rapidly determine what the object is, how to transact, who is selling it and what happens next.

### Adidas: editorial object preservation

The Adidas reference uses a neutral field, generous object-safe framing, understated typography and a clean size/fit transition. The product is never forced to compete with decorative chrome. This is a useful standard for Direct listings with strong photography.

## Current official-product benchmark themes

The researched products serve different jobs:

| Product family | Useful benchmark | Principle to borrow |
|---|---|---|
| Social resale | Depop | seller identity, low-friction discovery, social confidence |
| General marketplace | eBay | explicit Buy Now/offer/auction modes and decision completeness |
| Luxury resale | Vestiaire Collective | trust, authentication, provenance and premium restraint |
| Market-led collectibles | StockX | market truth, bids/asks, pricing context and transaction clarity |
| Curated auction | Catawiki | controlled urgency, lot curation, bid state and specialist trust |
| Fractional ownership | Rally / Masterworks | ownership position, offering evidence, disclosures and market status |

These are directional comparisons, not instructions to reproduce proprietary layouts.

## Gap analysis by family

### Direct

Current Thryftverse direction: media-first, overlaid identity, slim seller confidence, disclosures and discovery.

Gap: the fixed crop and image overlay can work against the object. Leading editorial commerce usually preserves product silhouette and makes the title/price readable independently of photo contrast. Direct should feel like a shoppable editorial object, not a dashboard placed over a photograph.

### Auction

Current direction: composed auction plaque with current bid, countdown, reserve/viewer state and actions.

Gap: the plaque is visually credible, but live truth is not. Without realtime bid change, reserve data and fulfilment closure, it performs “auction theatre.” The strongest auction experience makes freshness, bidder eligibility, winning/outbid state and terminal transition unmistakable.

### Co-Own

Current direction: premium market surface with price, book, position, rights and dossier.

Gap: visually closest to a differentiated flagship family, but the live asset contract cannot populate its rights/dossier and supports one image only. An ownership market must communicate evidence, legal/economic rights, custody and freshness more strongly than surface polish.

## Graphical enhancement principles

1. Preserve the object. Choose contain/object-safe crop from media metadata; reserve cover for editorial imagery with an approved focal point.
2. Give each family one signature instrument:
   - Direct: editorial price/condition/size strip.
   - Auction: live bid-and-time plaque.
   - Co-Own: price/position/liquidity instrument.
3. Use typography to establish hierarchy before containers. Reduce nested rounded surfaces.
4. Add meaningful micro-graphics only:
   - Direct: condition cue, size/fit chip, delivery arrival timeline.
   - Auction: restrained countdown progress, bid-change pulse and activity sparkline.
   - Co-Own: spread depth, settled-price sparkline and ownership allocation.
5. Keep trust information evidence-led. Authentication, protection, returns, custody and appraisals need a source, status and effective date.
6. Make terminal states visually final. Sold, won, lost, cancelled and settlement states should replace the live instrument rather than leave it in place with a different caption.
7. Use dark mode as an authored palette, not an inversion. Product imagery, dividers, status colours and sheets require direct review.

## First-viewport target

At 390 logical pixels, each family should communicate in one viewport:

- the object and its media position;
- title or asset identity;
- one primary price/market truth;
- seller/issuer confidence;
- current transaction eligibility;
- the primary action, without obscuring content.

Everything else should earn its place through progressive disclosure.
