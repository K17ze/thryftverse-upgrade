# Co-Own Market, Dossier and Media Prompt

## Goal

Turn the visually promising Co-Own screen into an operable, private and evidence-backed ownership market.

## Dossier

- Create versioned rights/offering records with effective dates.
- Model economic, voting, transfer, fee, exit and restriction terms.
- Add issuer/operator, custody/storage, provenance, authentication and appraisal evidence.
- Attach documents/media by verified IDs with status and version.
- Never backfill unknown facts as affirmative claims.

## Market

- Return atomic bid/ask depth with per-side limits.
- Include sequence, generated time, last book change and last execution.
- Stream deltas; resume or resnapshot on sequence gaps.
- Model open, closed, halted, stale and degraded states.
- Keep order/holding/cash invariants under concurrent partial fills.
- Return viewer position privately and asset-scoped.

## Media

Add canonical multi-media support. Permit product views, condition evidence, provenance detail and custody imagery, with clear labels. Documents must not masquerade as product imagery.

## Composition

- Market instrument combines price provenance, spread, freshness and viewer position.
- Order book remains compact.
- Rights and evidence are sober, versioned disclosures.
- Use minimal microcharts; avoid trading-terminal spectacle.
- Trading controls disappear or disable with a precise reason when unsafe.

## Tests

- complete/incomplete rights;
- rights-version change while viewing;
- public privacy/authorization;
- market gap/reconnect;
- crossed/stale book;
- partial fill/cancel/settlement;
- issuer versus holder permissions;
- mixed media and evidence takedown;
- halted/closed state.

## Completion

Do not claim Co-Own closure until an eligible non-issuer can trade a complete live asset, another holder’s private position is inaccessible, and the live book visibly detects disconnection/staleness.
