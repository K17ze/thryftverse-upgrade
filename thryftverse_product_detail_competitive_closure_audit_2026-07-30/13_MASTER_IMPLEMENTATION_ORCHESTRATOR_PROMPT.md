## Engineering principles

- Server computes commercial capabilities; client renders them.
- Missing facts remain missing.
- Every mutation is idempotent and has an unknown-outcome recovery path.
- Live interfaces expose freshness, sequence and connection health.
- Media is typed; URLs are never used to infer type.
- Published transactional evidence is versioned or frozen.
- Partial failures preserve working content and clearly block only unsafe actions.
- Shared primitives do not force shared page composition.
